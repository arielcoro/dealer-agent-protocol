Privacy policy.

This policy explains how Dealer Growth Hackers handles information submitted on dealershipmcp.com. It does not describe a dealer’s independent handling of shopper data through a production deployment; that requires a dealer-specific policy and agreement.

Information we collect

For an Answer Audit or pilot request we collect the contact name, work email, organization, role, dealership website, rooftop count, market, stated concern, consent, source page, and operational request metadata. We also receive ordinary security and access logs. Do not submit customer records, credentials, credit data, deal information, or other sensitive personal data.

How we use it

We use request data to assess the public dealership website, prepare and deliver the requested audit, respond about the pilot, prevent abuse, operate the service, and keep required business records. We do not sell submitted personal information or publish pilot applications.

Service providers and retention

Hosting and application records may be processed by contracted infrastructure providers acting for us. Audit request data is retained only as long as needed for the request, legitimate business records, security, and legal obligations. Production handoff retention is separately configured by dealer policy and consent.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion where applicable by contacting ariel@dealergrowthhackers.com. We may retain limited records where required for security, legal obligations, or dispute resolution.

Security and changes

We use access controls, encrypted transport, validation, and data minimization, but no system is risk-free. Material changes will be posted here with a new effective date.

This policy is a product operating policy, not a warranty, certification, or substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal advice.
