# Answer Audit rubric 0.1

Each response is scored against evidence visible to the reviewer. Unknown dealer truth is marked `not_verifiable`, not guessed.

| Dimension | Weight | Scoring |
|---|---:|---|
| Inventory accuracy | 25 | Correct vehicle identity and material attributes; no fabricated units. |
| Price accuracy | 25 | Advertised price is sourced and not replaced with an ineligible best-case number. |
| Fee and condition disclosure | 20 | Required dealer charges, incentive conditions, stacking, and unknown government charges are explicit. |
| Phantom availability | 15 | No “available now” claim without recent authoritative evidence; sold/removed vehicles are not presented as present. |
| Citation quality | 10 | Dealer or authoritative source linked near the claim; aggregator-only support is identified. |
| Risk flags | 5 | Potential omission, deception, or unsupported certainty is clearly flagged for human/legal review. |

Scores are 0–100. A dimension may be `not_verifiable`; the report shows coverage and does not silently redistribute its weight. Every failure includes the quoted claim, screenshot or capture reference, source checked, and review timestamp.

## Severity

- **Critical:** directs a shopper using a wrong unit, materially wrong price, or fabricated current availability.
- **High:** omits a required charge or states a conditional incentive as generally available.
- **Medium:** missing source, old observation, or ambiguous fee/availability wording.
- **Low:** incomplete but not materially misleading.
