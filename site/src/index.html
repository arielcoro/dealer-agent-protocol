01 / Purpose

Five questions every shopping agent should answer correctly.

  1. 01

    Who is selling the vehicle, and at which rooftop?

  2. 02

    Which specific vehicles are published for sale?

  3. 03

    Is the vehicle available—according to whom, and as of when?

  4. 04

    What price and required dealer charges apply to everyone?

  5. 05

    Which discounts may apply, under what conditions, and in which combinations?

02 / Boundary

Retail facts in.
Dealer systems out.

A gateway may read from a feed, website platform, inventory service, pricing source, or DMS. Those integrations stay behind the gateway. The protocol exposes a normalized retail catalog—not generic system access.

+ Version 0.1

  • Published vehicle inventory
  • Vehicle details and media
  • Availability and freshness
  • Advertised price
  • Required dealer charges
  • Discount and incentive rules
  • Provenance and uncertainty

− Not version 0.1

  • DMS or CRM access
  • Customer records and leads
  • Appointments and holds
  • Desking and deal jackets
  • Credit and lender decisions
  • Payments and contracts
  • Service, parts, and marketing

03 / Implement

Small surface.
Hard guarantees.

The mandatory bundle contains six read tools across four profiles. Every result carries typed money, source lineage, freshness, authority, and explicit uncertainty.
