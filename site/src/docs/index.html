In one sentence

Dealer Agent Protocol is an open, read-only contract that lets AI agents discover dealer-published vehicles, verify availability, and explain retail pricing with source, freshness, authority, and uncertainty.

Version 0.1 scope

The first release defines one focused retail read: who is selling the vehicle, which vehicles are published, whether a specific vehicle is available, and how its price and offers should be explained.

Core retail factWhat the agent learns
Dealer and rooftop identityWho is publishing the inventory and retail offer.
Published inventory and vehicle factsWhich exact vehicles are being presented for sale.
Authoritative availabilityWhether a specific vehicle is currently available, according to the designated authority.
Pricing and required chargesWhich amounts apply to every buyer in the disclosed context.
