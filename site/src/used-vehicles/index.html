Used Vehicle Retail Profile · 0.1

A used car is not just a VIN and a price.

Mileage, days in inventory, history reports, title, inspection, certification, warranty, and reconditioning all affect the retail answer. The protocol makes every one traceable.

The differentiatorNot more fields alone. Every used-car claim keeps its source, observation time, sharing authority, and unresolved conflicts.

One unit. Five kinds of evidence.

U2 · Inventory age

“47 days” is incomplete. The protocol shows which clock is running.

  • 01Stocked

    When the dealer accepted the unit into owned or consigned retail inventory.

  • 02First listed

    When the unit first appeared in a public retail listing. This can be later than stocked.

  • 03Age basis

    Which event produced the reported age—or whether a source supplied only days in stock.

  • 04As of

    The timestamp against which complete elapsed days were calculated.

A statement an agent can defend: “This vehicle has been in dealer inventory for 47 complete days as of September 1, based on the dealer inventory system.”
