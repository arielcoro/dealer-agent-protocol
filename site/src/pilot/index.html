Put your retail truth in front of AI agents.

What this isAn evaluation pilot for dealer-published vehicle inventory, availability, pricing, required charges, and conditional incentives. It does not require customer records or broad agent access to dealer systems.
01 / Good fit

Designed for a dealer team that wants to lead, not wait.

The founding cohort is intentionally small. We are looking for operators willing to define approved sources, challenge the answers, and help shape a practical open contract for automotive retail.

  1. 1One starting rooftop

    A focused environment with active retail inventory and a clear internal owner.

  2. 2Approved retail sources

    Website, feed, pricing, incentive, or availability data your team already publishes or controls.

  3. 3Answer reviewers

    Someone who can judge whether an agent described a vehicle and offer accurately.

  4. 4No customer data

    The core retail read works with vehicle, dealer, price, offer, provenance, and freshness facts.

What selected dealers receive

A controlled path from source map to agent-ready answers.

01

Retail truth map

Document which source owns inventory, price, charges, incentives, and availability.

02

Private gateway evaluation

Map one rooftop behind a restricted Dealer Agent Gateway environment.

03

Answer review

Test real shopping questions, edge cases, stale data, and conditional offers with your team.

04

Readiness report

Receive conformance evidence, gaps, decisions, and a recommendation for the next stage.
