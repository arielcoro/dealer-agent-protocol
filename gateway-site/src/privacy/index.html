Summary

The project provides no user accounts, contact forms, advertising, payment processing, or behavioral analytics. We do not sell personal information. The public endpoint contains synthetic vehicles and should never receive credentials, personal data, confidential dealer data, or production records.

Information processed

Hosting and security infrastructure may process IP address, date and time, requested URL, user agent, routing data, and security events to deliver and protect the service. MCP requests may also involve request bodies, protocol metadata, timestamps, response status, and errors needed to respond, rate-limit, secure, and debug the fixture.

Contributions made through GitHub are processed and published by GitHub under its own policies.

Service providers

Cloudflare hosts and protects the sites and may process network data under its Privacy Policy. Google Fonts supplies web fonts and may receive browser and network information under the Google Privacy Policy. GitHub hosts public project collaboration under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Cookies and tracking

The project does not intentionally set advertising cookies or perform cross-site behavioral tracking. Infrastructure providers may use essential delivery and security mechanisms.

Use, disclosure, and retention

Operational information is used to provide and secure the service, investigate failures or abuse, and improve interoperability. It may be disclosed when required by law, to protect the project or others, or to providers performing those functions. It is not sold or shared for targeted advertising and is retained only as reasonably needed, subject to provider settings and legal requirements.

Rights, children, and contact

Privacy rights vary by location. The developer resources are not directed to children under 13. For a privacy request, open a repository issue without sensitive information and ask for a private channel. Security reports should use private vulnerability reporting.

Changes

Material changes will be posted here with a revised effective date. Qualified legal review should occur before the project adds accounts, analytics, commercial services, or production data.
