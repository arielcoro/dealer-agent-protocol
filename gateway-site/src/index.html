01 / Capability surface

Six tools.
Zero invented facts.

The public pilot exposes six experimental retail-read tools. Each response keeps the rooftop, source, observation time, authority, and uncertainty attached; it does not claim full Core conformance.

  1. 01
    dealeragent.discovery.get_manifest

    Discover profiles, scopes, resources, and conformance evidence.

  2. 02
    dealeragent.dealer.get

    Resolve the dealer organization and its retail rooftops.

  3. 03
    dealeragent.inventory.search

    Search dealer-published vehicles with typed filters and cursors.

  4. 04
    dealeragent.inventory.get_vehicle

    Read one vehicle without treating the listing as a promise.

  5. 05
    dealeragent.inventory.verify_availability

    Return the latest evidence and require a human check until a real-time authority is connected.

  6. 06
    dealeragent.pricing.get_disclosure

    Separate advertised price, required charges, and conditional offers.

02 / Trust boundary

The adapter stays behind the line.

The Gateway connects to approved inventory sources and can add authoritative pricing and availability sources as dealers provide them. Agents receive normalized retail facts—not generic access to operational systems.
