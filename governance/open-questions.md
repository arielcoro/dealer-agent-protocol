# Open questions

Open questions are managed outside the normative specification. Each item has an accountable owner and review date. “Open” does not weaken current 0.1 behavior; the published decision remains in force until changed through governance.

| Question | Current 0.1 decision | Owner | Review by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Per-record signed attestations | Not standardized; gateway-level conformance and provenance are sufficient for 0.1. | Security working group | 2026-10-01 |
| Neutral profile identifier registry | Reverse-domain extensions; `dealeragent.*` reserved by this project. | Specification editor | 2026-10-15 |
| Zero- and three-decimal currency vectors | Minor-unit rule is normative; expand vectors before candidate. | Pricing working group | 2026-09-30 |
| Independent implementations | Candidate requires two servers and two clients against the same suite. | Conformance working group | 2026-12-01 |
| Source-specific freshness targets | Five bands are normative; tighter source classes await pilot measurements. | Availability working group | 2026-10-15 |
| Incentive eligibility vocabulary | Existing enum plus `other`; expand only with cross-market evidence. | Pricing working group | 2026-11-01 |
| Dealer co-chair | Seat remains open until an independent dealer accepts it; no placeholder will be named. | Creator | 2026-11-01 |
