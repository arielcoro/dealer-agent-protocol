# Working groups

Working groups prepare proposals and tests; they do not bypass the public decision process in `GOVERNANCE.md`.

| Group | Charter | Initial chairing |
|---|---|---|
| Pricing and disclosure | Price classification, fees, incentives, eligibility, stacking, and completeness scoring. | Specification editor; dealer co-chair seat open |
| Availability and provenance | Authority classes, freshness bands, source lineage, and false-availability prevention. | Specification editor; dealer co-chair seat open |
| Consent and handoff | Consent binding, PII minimization, ADF delivery, replay protection, and retention. | Security maintainer; dealer privacy counsel seat open |
| Conformance | Requirement IDs, executable vectors, claims, signatures, and badge rules. | Test maintainer; independent implementer seat open |

Membership is public and contribution-based. Employees of Dealer Growth Hackers may participate, but product priorities do not decide protocol requirements. Material affiliations and conflicts must be disclosed with proposals.
