# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to making participation in this project respectful and free from
harassment for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or
commercial affiliation. Debate the technical proposal and its evidence, not the
person presenting it.

## Expected behavior

- Be precise, constructive, and open about uncertainty.
- Disclose material conflicts of interest.
- Respect dealer, consumer, security, and privacy concerns.
- Give credit and accept correction.
- Avoid intimidation, personal attacks, discriminatory language, and unwanted
  sexual attention.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers. Do not
publish sensitive details. Maintainers may edit or remove content, pause a
discussion, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to
protect contributors and the review process. Enforcement decisions should be
documented to the extent privacy and safety permit.

This code applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly.
It is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
