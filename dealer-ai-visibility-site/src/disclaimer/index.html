Methodology disclaimer.

Dealer AI Visibility measures observable public signals associated with machine access, understanding, citation, authority, and brand connection. The score is diagnostic and directional.

It is not a live ranking check

AI answers vary by platform, model, user, location, prompt, date, account context, and source availability. A strong score does not guarantee a mention. A weak score does not prove that a dealership will never appear.

Automated findings require judgment

Website platforms may serve different content to different crawlers. Public sources may be incomplete. Findings should be verified before implementation, especially where OEM, legal, accessibility, privacy, or advertising requirements apply.

Named platforms

ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, and Bing Copilot are trademarks or products of their respective owners. Their names are used only to describe the category of systems the analysis is intended to help dealerships understand.

Commercial relationships

Dealer AI Visibility may recommend a deeper Dealer AEO Audit or DealershipMCP pilot when the results support it. Those services are operated or promoted by the same team. The free score does not require purchasing either service.
