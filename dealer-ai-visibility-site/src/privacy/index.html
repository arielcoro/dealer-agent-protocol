Privacy policy.

Dealer AI Visibility is operated by Dealer Growth Hackers. This policy explains the information collected when you scan a dealership website, view a report, or request follow-up.

Information we collect

A scan may include the dealership name, public website URL, optional competitor URL, approximate city derived from the request, scan results, and technical request information such as IP address, browser, and referral source. If you request follow-up, we may collect your name, work email, phone number, and dealership.

How we use it

We use the information to run and improve the scan, produce shareable reports, prevent abuse, respond to requests, measure product performance, and understand dealership AI visibility trends. We do not sell personal information.

Service providers

We use Cloudflare for hosting and security, Supabase for scan processing and report storage, Google Analytics for aggregate usage measurement, and website-performance or crawling providers needed to analyze public pages. Their processing is governed by their own policies.

Public reports

Scan reports are accessible to anyone with the report link and describe public dealership website signals. Do not submit private customer, employee, financial, or dealership-system information.

Retention and choices

We retain scan and inquiry data as reasonably necessary to operate, secure, and improve the service. You may request access, correction, deletion, or marketing opt-out by emailing hello@dealeraivisibility.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to hello@dealeraivisibility.com.
