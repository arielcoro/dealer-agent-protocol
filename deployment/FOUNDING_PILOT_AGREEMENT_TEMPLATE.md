# Founding dealer pilot agreement — discussion template

This template is not executed and is not legal advice. Dealer Growth Hackers and the participating dealer should have counsel review and sign a completed agreement before connecting nonpublic sources or accepting customer contact data.

## 1. Parties and rooftops

Identify Dealer Growth Hackers, the legal dealer entity, authorized signers, covered rooftops, public websites, and approved technical contacts.

## 2. Fourteen-day pilot scope

- Baseline AI Answer Audit.
- Read-only mapping of approved inventory, pricing, fee, incentive, and availability sources.
- Dealer review and approval of publication policy.
- Synthetic conformance first; production enablement only after operational gates pass.
- Optional handoff test only with approved disclosure, retention, CRM destination, and test contacts until written production approval.

## 3. Data and access boundaries

List each source, fields, authority class, refresh SLA, credential owner, subprocessor, region, and retention. Prohibit generic DMS/CRM access, customer-record reads, credit, lender, payment, contract, and deal-jacket data. Read credentials must be least-privileged and revocable.

## 4. Consent and handoff

State accepted purposes and channels, exact disclosure/version, required and optional data categories, subject-binding method, binding lifetime, single-use enforcement, ADF destination, response commitment, retention ceiling, deletion path, and incident contact. No production contact may be forwarded without a valid consent binding.

## 5. Security and operations

Define tenant isolation, encryption, access control, audit logging, alerting, rate limits, backups, incident notice, credential rotation, change control, uptime target, rollback owner, and termination procedure.

## 6. Measurement and publicity

Define baseline and endline measures, dealer truth verification, false-availability review, fee/disclosure review, and acceptance criteria. No dealer name, logo, result, conformance claim, or case study is public without separate written approval.

## 7. Commercial and legal terms

State that the founding pilot price, if free, does not waive later pricing; identify any expenses; confidentiality; ownership; open-source boundaries; warranties; liability; indemnity; governing law; term; termination; data return/deletion; and survival. Dealer Agent Protocol remains Apache-2.0 and independently implementable; DealershipMCP services are commercial.
